# 🎥 90-Second Portfolio Video Walkthrough Script

Recording a 90-second Loom or Screen Studio video walkthrough of your dashboard and pinning it to your **LinkedIn Featured section** and **GitHub README** will put you in the top 5% of all applicants.

---

### Step-by-Step Recording Script

#### ⏱️ [00:00 - 00:20] Hook & Business Problem
* **Screen:** Start on **Tab 1: Executive Command**.
* **You Say:**
  > *"Hi everyone! As data analysts, we often build dashboards that show what happened, but rarely help executives decide what to do next. Today, I'm showcasing **The Revenue & Customer Retention Intelligence Engine**, an end-to-end analytics platform tracking over \$130 million in active B2B SaaS revenue across 5,000 accounts."*

#### ⏱️ [00:20 - 00:40] Cohort Retention & SQL Power
* **Screen:** Click on **Tab 2: Cohort Retention Matrix**.
* **You Say:**
  > *"First, using advanced SQL recursive CTEs, I built this 12-month cohort retention heatmap. You can see our steepest drop-off occurs in Month 1—losing about 11% of newly signed accounts before stabilizing around Month 4. This immediately told us our biggest leak was during unassisted customer onboarding."*

#### ⏱️ [00:40 - 01:05] Machine Learning & Churn Diagnostics
* **Screen:** Click on **Tab 3: Churn Diagnostics & Explainability**.
* **You Say:**
  > *"Next, rather than guessing why customers leave, I trained a Gradient Boosted Classifier. As shown in this feature importance chart, ticket resolution time exceeding 48 hours was the number one leading predictor of churn—even more impactful than pricing or product feature count."*

#### ⏱️ [01:05 - 01:25] AI Copilot & Action Playbook
* **Screen:** Click on **Tab 4: At-Risk Watchlist**, select an enterprise company, and click **'Generate Tailored Retention Playbook'**.
* **You Say:**
  > *"Here's where the decision intelligence comes in: For high-value accounts at risk, our integrated AI Copilot generates an immediate 24-hour SLA emergency response and a 30-day account stabilization plan for the customer success team."*

#### ⏱️ [01:25 - 01:30] Call to Action
* **Screen:** Click on **Tab 5: What-If Revenue Simulator**, slide the churn reduction to 2.5%, showing **\$3.3M saved**.
* **You Say:**
  > *"By testing operational improvements in this scenario simulator, saving just 2.5% in churn protects over \$3.3M in annual ARR. Check out the complete SQL code and repo linked below!"*
